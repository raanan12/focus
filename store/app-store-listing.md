**Description:**
Focus is the simplest way to keep notes and tasks — and to share them.

• Colorful notes and checklists, organized the way you like
• Share any note with a partner, family member or teammate — by e-mail, share code or link
• Everyone sees changes instantly and can add tasks
• Each partner gets their own color, so you always know who added what
• Due-date reminders so nothing slips through
• Works offline and syncs when you're back online
• Beautiful light and dark modes, in Hebrew, English and Arabic (full right-to-left support)
• Sign in with Apple or Google — no passwords to remember

Try Focus free for 14 days. After the trial, a monthly subscription keeps you creating and sharing new notes. People you invite never pay.

Subscription: Focus Premium, monthly. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID settings.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label") answers
Data collected, all **linked to the user**, **not used for tracking**, purpose: App Functionality:
- Contact Info → Email Address, Name
- User Content → Other User Content (notes, tasks)
- Identifiers → User ID
- Purchases → Purchase History (subscription status)
Not collected: location, contacts, photos, health, browsing history, advertising data, diagnostics from third-party SDKs.
"Do you or your third-party partners use data for tracking?" → No.
